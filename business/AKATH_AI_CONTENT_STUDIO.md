# AKATH AI Content Studio

## Mission
Launch a low-capital service business under AKATH that sells AI-assisted short-form video and music-content production to Thai creators, small businesses, and brands.

## Why this business
- It can start with existing AI/music/video capabilities and near-zero inventory.
- The Thai freelance market already has active AI-video demand; Fastwork currently lists AI video as a service category and reports AI-video services starting around ฿300, while individual listings commonly package 30–60 second AI videos from roughly ฿500–฿1,500. Source: Fastwork market pages reviewed 2026-09-06.
- The first offer can be delivered digitally, so there is no shipping or physical stock requirement.

## Initial offer
### AI Short Video Starter — ฿500
- 30-second vertical video
- 5–6 AI-generated scenes
- Basic captions
- One revision
- Delivery target: 48 hours after complete brief/assets

### AI Short Video Pro — ฿1,500
- 60-second vertical video
- Storyboard + scene prompts
- Captions and basic sound design
- Two revisions
- Delivery target: 3 days after complete brief/assets

### Music Content Pack — ฿2,000
- One short-form visualizer or promotional music clip
- Vertical + horizontal exports
- Basic cover/title treatment
- One revision

## Customer acquisition
1. Publish the offer on an available Thai freelance marketplace/profile.
2. Use the existing AKATH/AERIS portfolio as proof-of-work.
3. Target urgent creator/SME requests for AI video, Shorts, Reels and music promotion.
4. Respond with a fixed-price package first; upsell only after the first completed order.

## Operating model
K = final authority and customer/financial approval.
AX = sales routing, brief intake, production orchestration, QA and delivery tracking.
MUSE = creative direction.
Gemini QC = technical/content QA.
NOVA = brief, file and delivery administration.
PC2 = production executor.

## Revenue gate
No revenue is counted until a customer payment/order is actually confirmed. A published service page or proposal is a lead-generation result, not revenue.

## First 3-hour objective
- Publish a usable offer page.
- Prepare one portfolio-driven sales message.
- Identify at least three current demand signals/leads.
- Establish the intake → production → QA → delivery path.
- Do not fabricate an order, customer, or payment.

## Success metrics
- Leads contacted
- Replies
- Quotes sent
- Orders confirmed
- Cash received
- Delivery turnaround
- Gross revenue
