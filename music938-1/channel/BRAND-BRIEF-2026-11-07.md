# Brief: new AERIS Music channel identity (avatar + banner)

## Who we are
AERIS Music — a Thai AI music label. YouTube channel `@aerismusicth` (ch3), plus a
Facebook Page, TikTok `@aeris.music4` and Shopee. Content today is Thai songs
(luk thung / mor lam / pop / phluea chiwit / sad songs) with karaoke lyrics, posted
daily. Revenue is affiliate (Shopee) + future AdSense. Goal: 1,000 subs and $500.

Current identity ("Calm Minimalist Sanctuary", adopted 2026-11-02): twilight-indigo
field, one continuous stroke that is both a sine soundwave and the letter A, a warm
coral accent, a thin silver ring. Tagline "เพลงไทยเพื่อชีวิต · คาราโอเกะ".

## The brief K wants us to take seriously
K wants the channel to read as **indie / warm / easy-listening**, with **headphone
and audio-gear content**, and an image that says "airy, open, relaxed, warm" —
matching the name AERIS (air / breeze). The brief is from an AI YouTube design
consult and asks for:

1. Avatar, two options — (a) a minimal "A" monogram fused with a soundwave or a
   simple headphone shape, modern sans-serif, one flat colour field (midnight blue
   OR warm cream) with a white/pastel mark; (b) a soundwave/note that flows like a
   breeze, with a minimal headphone icon.
2. Banner, "Acoustic & Breeze" mood — lo-fi / vector illustration: a window with
   morning light, a wooden desk, headphones and a coffee cup. Earth tones + pastel
   (cream, soft terracotta, indigo, muted green). Centre-safe: wordmark, a short
   tagline ("เพลงอบอุ่น ฟังสบาย & Gadget สายฟังเพลง" / "Your Daily Acoustic & Indie
   Vibes"), and a posting schedule line.
3. Brand continuity: the same palette and type must appear on thumbnails and Shorts.

## The tension we need advice on
- The channel's actual output is Thai songs + karaoke, not gear reviews. A
  headphone-led identity may promise reviews we do not publish, and may attract
  viewers who leave. Is that a real risk, and how do we carry "audio gear" without
  the channel reading as a tech-review channel?
- We already have a soundwave-A mark and a committed indigo family. Do we **evolve**
  it (add warmth, a headphone cue, keep indigo) or **pivot** to the cream/earth
  palette? A pivot mid-growth loses recognition; an evolution may not deliver the
  "warm / airy" feeling K asked for.
- An avatar is seen at ~88px. What actually survives at that size: a monogram, a
  headphone glyph, or a note? Should the avatar and the banner share one mark?
- The banner's guaranteed-visible area on every device is only the centre strip
  (1546x423 of 2560x1440). The brief wants several text lines. What must be dropped?

## What we want back
A concrete recommendation we can render in code (Pillow, no stock assets, free
tools only): the palette (hex), the mark concept, the type treatment, and the exact
elements that belong in the avatar vs the banner safe strip. Say what you would
*reject* from the brief and why.
