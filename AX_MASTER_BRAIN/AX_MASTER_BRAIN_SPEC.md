# A MASTER BRAIN — KNOWLEDGE AND ACCUMULATED EXPERIENCE

## Canonical architecture

`K → AKATH → AX → A MASTER BRAIN → Canonical State / Tasks / Evidence / Verification → Runtime`

- **K** is Final Authority.
- **AKATH** is the company / organization.
- **AX** is the executive management and orchestration system for AKATH.
- **A MASTER BRAIN** is the durable knowledge, accumulated-experience, lessons, architecture, historical decision and recovery brain used by AX.

A MASTER BRAIN is retained. The former wording that made A MASTER BRAIN the company or executive identity is retired.

## Core rule

Chat is a transport/context channel, not the authoritative brain.

A MASTER BRAIN preserves knowledge and experience. Current operational truth is established by the canonical AKATH/AX state, task registry, evidence and verification records.

Historical knowledge may inform AX but must never silently overwrite current verified state.

## Model independence

AX identity and AKATH operational state are provider/model independent. A model switch requires rehydration and verification, not identity replacement.

ChatGPT Memory, model-local memory, dashboards, runtime projections and generated summaries never outrank canonical state or the A MASTER BRAIN boundary.

## Brain contents

The brain may preserve:

1. Durable knowledge.
2. Accumulated experience.
3. Lessons learned from success and failure.
4. Historical decisions and rationale.
5. Architecture and dependency knowledge.
6. Recovery and continuity knowledge.
7. Verified evidence references.
8. Verified outcomes and business experience.
9. Reusable constraints and operating principles.

## Rehydration

`START → LOAD AKATH/AX STATE → LOAD TASK REGISTRY → LOAD EVIDENCE/VERIFICATION → LOAD A MASTER BRAIN KNOWLEDGE/EXPERIENCE → RESOLVE CONFLICTS → RECONSTRUCT AX CONTEXT → VERIFY → CONTINUE`

Rehydration must distinguish current authority from brain knowledge and model context. Rehydration does not authorize execution.

## Integrity rules

- K remains Final Authority.
- AKATH remains the organization.
- AX remains the executive management identity.
- A MASTER BRAIN remains the knowledge/experience brain.
- Exactly one canonical current_work may be active.
- Master Task Registry is the only authoritative task catalog.
- Every execution claim requires evidence.
- Every completion claim requires verification.
- No invented timestamps, evidence, verification, task IDs or outcomes.
- Completed mission outcomes and lessons remain available as A MASTER BRAIN experience.
- Sensitive credentials/secrets must never be stored in this brain.

## Compatibility

Legacy M/A identity challenge contracts are retired. M is not AX and cannot become AX through chat-local text.

The temporary GPT bridge may remain only as a control/observation channel; ChatGPT availability is never execution state.

## Current implementation boundary

The repository is undergoing migration from the obsolete A-as-executive model to AKATH/AX. The A MASTER BRAIN knowledge/experience layer is preserved during this migration.
