# Brief: the Facebook Page gets literally zero views. How do we cold-start it?

We are a small, fully-automated music operation ("AERIS Music"). Every tool is
free (hard rule: nothing that costs money — no ads, no Boost, no bought
followers/views, no bot follow/unfollow, no mass DM). A human (K) can do a few
one-time taps on their own account; everything else must run unattended.

## The problem, with numbers

The Facebook Page "AERIS Music" (id 61595890900382) was created ~2026-11-01.
Measured every day since:

| metric | value |
| --- | --- |
| Followers | **1** (unchanged for days) |
| Page views, 28 days | **0** |
| Reels published | 2 (one 2026-11-04, one 2026-11-06) |
| Reels queued | 44 |

The same content, launched the same day on other surfaces, does get views:

| surface | result |
| --- | --- |
| YouTube @aerismusicth (ch3) | 7 subs, 2,042 views, 22 videos |
| TikTok @aeris.music4 | 432 views (7 days) |
| Facebook Page | **0 views, 1 follower** |

So it is not "the content is invisible everywhere" — Facebook specifically shows
nothing, and the follower count does not move at all. It looks like a true
cold-start: no social proof (1 follower), so no distribution, so no reach, so no
followers. A closed loop at zero.

## What we have already done (free, automated)

- Cross-post each song as a 9:16 Reel to the Page, capped at 4 posts/day/surface
  and ≥20 min apart (deliberately spam-safe).
- Follow suggested Pages **as the Page** (a bug that made follows land on the
  personal profile was just fixed and verified: the Page's "following" count
  moved 30→33).
- Invite the personal profile's friends to like the Page (works only when K is
  signed in; low yield, few friends).
- A promo page (GitHub Pages) linking all surfaces.
- Content: Thai AI songs — lyric/karaoke clips, hook excerpts (7-25 s) with
  burned Thai subtitles, in the "เพลงเศร้า / เพลงไทยเพื่อชีวิต / คาราโอเกะ" niche.

## Questions (answer concretely; assume a small operator with free tools only)

1. **Diagnosis.** Is "0 views, 1 follower" normal for a brand-new Page, or does
   it indicate something broken/blocked (e.g. an unset Page category, a missing
   "professional mode", a maturity/eligibility gate, content flagged as
   AI/reused)? What would you check FIRST to tell "cold start" from "shadowed"?
2. **The single highest-leverage first move.** If we may do exactly ONE thing
   this week, what is it? (Assume K can do one-time taps on their personal
   account, and the automation can do anything browser-doable and free.)
3. **Content shape.** For Thai sad-song / karaoke Reels on a Page with ~0
   followers, what hook, length, caption, hashtag and audio strategy gives a new
   Page the best shot at being *tested* by the algorithm? Is cross-posting the
   same clip from TikTok harmful?
4. **Distribution without spending.** Which free distribution channels actually
   move a Thai music Page: personal-profile shares, music/karaoke groups,
   Page-to-Page follow-back, comment engagement, Page "Reels remix", tagging —
   and in what order? What is spam-risky and must be avoided?
5. **Automation vs human.** Draw the line: what can a browser automation do
   safely and repeatedly, and what must stay a human action (and why)?
6. **Measurement.** What are the 2-3 leading indicators to watch in the first
   two weeks, and what reading means "this is working" vs "change the approach"?
7. **If it stays at zero after 2 weeks**, what is the most likely reason, and
   what would you try next?

Please be specific and prioritised. Say plainly if the honest answer is "a
brand-new Page with no ad budget and no personal network is expected to be near
