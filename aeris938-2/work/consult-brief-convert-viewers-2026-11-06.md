# Brief: make people come to us and follow the Page (no outbound automation)

## K's decision (2026-11-06)

K accepted that we will not chase people (no automated follows/invites — a
five-advisor panel already ruled that ban-bait). Instead: **build the places
people come to, and make following the obvious next step.** "ถ้าไม่ดันเพจ ก็ต้อง
สร้างเพจให้คนเข้ามาหาเรา" — make the Page a destination, not a thing we push.
