## Facts (measured 2026-11-07, read as the Page)

- Page id 61595890900382, opened ~1 Nov 2026. **1 follower, 0 comments.**
- 8 posts so far (Reels + image posts), all Thai music (ลูกทุ่ง / เพื่อชีวิต /
  คาราโอเกะ / เพลงเศร้า).
- Page views: **861 in 28 days, 99.9% from non-followers.** A single Reel reached
  **129 views**. So Facebook IS distributing the clips to strangers.
- Same clips are cross-posted to TikTok (@aeris.music4, 0 followers, 441 views/7d)
  and YouTube (@aerismusicth, 7 subs, 2,042 views).
- Shopee affiliate link is in every caption; **0 clicks, 0 orders.**

## The problem

Views happen but do not convert. 861 strangers saw the Page and one followed.
Posting more clips at the same quality will not change that. What free change
converts a Reel *viewer* into a *follower* and then a *click*?

## Constraints (hard)

1. **FREE only** — no paid ads, no paid tools.
2. The account must not be risked: an earlier panel said do **not** automate
   outbound engagement (follow-for-follow, friend invites, commenting on other
   Pages, cold DMs) — Meta's spam policy keys on the pattern and there is no safe
   per-day rate. So recommend only content/creative changes, or a human-tapped
   action, not an automated outbound bot.
3. Thai audience, mobile-first.
4. One operator (K) who can tap, not sit and type all day.
