## 3. Thumbnails (weak signal)

Medians: brightness 38.1 vs 50.0, contrast 47.3 vs 51.1, saturation 0.36 vs 0.41
(top vs bottom) — the better-performing thumbnails are **darker and less
saturated**, consistent with the moody night-street palette. The differences are
small and n=20; treat as a hint, not a rule.

## 4. What cannot be measured yet

- **Impressions / CTR** are not returned by this token's scope (`impressions`
  metric → HTTP 400), so thumbnail A/B cannot be judged from the API. Studio
  shows them; the API does not.
- The channel-level average view duration (48 s) does not reconcile with
  minutes/views (16.3 s). Per-video rows are internally consistent (269 × 305 s =
  867 min), so the per-video table is what this report trusts.
- 66 of 112 uploads are old enough to rank; the rest are too new.

## 5. Actions this supports

1. **Volumes are the hours engine** — 55.5% of all watch time from one 57-minute
   video. Keep the weekly volume, and keep the "รวมเพลง … (เลือกเพลงฟังได้)"
   title shape, which is the shape that produced it.
2. **Clips: keep ≤ 60 s, put a question in the title, keep `#Shorts`.**
3. **Stop publishing 3–4 minute single-song videos.** They are the worst bucket
   and they cannot recover once the Shorts feed rejects them.
4. **Drop the em-dash descriptive titles** — they are 7× more common in the
   bottom group.
5. **Skip thumbnail A/B for now**: without impressions in the API and with this
   traffic, a test cannot reach significance. Revisit when a video has enough
   impressions in Studio to split.
