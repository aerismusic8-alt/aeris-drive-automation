## Flow Music CANNOT clone a voice — proven, not assumed
The composer's "Add audio or image" → "Upload audio file" takes a clip (≤40 MB)
and shows it as a reference chip. But on send the producer rejects a clip that
contains vocals:

> "Transcribed lyrics for this track might violate our content policies.
> To upload it, remove the vocals." → [Cancel] [Create a similar original track]
> [Remove vocals & upload]

`Create a similar original track` does **not** use the voice — it discards the
reference and writes a fresh song (the session returns to the starter screen).
So: no voice cloning in Flow. Do not spend time re-testing this.

Also, sending any producer message is `POST /__api/conversation` and it **429s**
if you retry too fast — space sends out by minutes, and poll for the message to
land rather than assuming one click worked. `session/new` also throws
"Something went wrong"; the working URL is `/session?t=true`.

## The free path that works: stem swap + kNN-VC
1. Flow exposes the 4 stems of every clip: `GET /__api/stems/clip/<id>` with the
   app's Supabase bearer (see `flow_song.py::_stems`). Grab `vocals.m4a`.
2. Convert the vocal to K's voice with **kNN-VC** (bshall/knn-vc): zero-shot,
   no training, WavLM-large + HiFi-GAN, CPU-only.
3. Mix the converted vocal back over drums+bass+other.

Result for song 01: converted vocal **F0 118 Hz** vs K's 112 Hz. A/B page at
`voice-preview/` (served on 12000; dies on pod recycle).

### kNN-VC on this pod — the four things that bit
- Install into a venv under `/workspace` (`voice-clone/vc-venv`), torch **CPU**
  from `download.pytorch.org/whl/cpu`, plus `typing_extensions` (torch import
  fails without it after a recycle). `TORCH_HOME=/workspace/ax-tools/torch-cache`
  so the 1.3 GB of checkpoints survive a recycle.
- `torch.hub.load(...)` defaults `device='cuda'` and **asserts** on a CPU-only
  build — pass `device="cpu"` explicitly.
- torchaudio 2.11 routes `load`/`save` through **torchcodec** (not installed) →
  monkey-patch both to use `soundfile`. `torchaudio.functional` (used inside
  `match`) is fine.
- **WavLM-large OOM-kills the whole pod** on a multi-minute clip (attention is
  quadratic; it took the pod down mid-run at 02:32). Encode in ~15 s chunks —
  speaker features are local, so chunked encoding is faithful. Keep the
  reference ≤60 s. Peak RSS then stays under ~7 GB of the 15 GB.
- `get_matching_set` runs `torchaudio` VAD which returns an empty tensor on this
  version → call `get_features(..., vad_trigger_level=0)` instead.
