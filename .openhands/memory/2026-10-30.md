## The tofu-text clip bug, and the near-black background (2026-10-30, later)

K watched the released clips: "ตัวหนังสือในคลิปอ่านไม่ออก ภาษาอะไร ภาพพืนหลังไม่มี".
Both halves were real, and both were **silent** — the mp4 still played, so nothing
downstream ever failed.

**The text was tofu.** Every ASS style names `Noto Sans Thai`, but the image ships
**no Thai font at all** (`fc-list :charset=0e01` is empty; only Noto Mono/Sans Mono).
libass substituted a Latin face and drew one box per syllable. `covers.py` had the
same bug from the other direction: it pointed at
`/usr/share/fonts/truetype/noto/NotoSansThai-Regular.ttf`, which does not exist, and
its `except OSError` fell through to Pillow's **bitmap default** — which also has no
Thai glyphs. Two independent paths, one symptom.
Fix: the font is bundled at `music938-1/fonts/NotoSansThai.ttf` (variable font, so
`set_variation_by_name("Bold")` replaces a second file; `scripts/install-thai-font.sh`
fetches it). libass only looks in a directory when told: **`fontsdir=` is required**,
naming the family is not enough. Both paths now raise on a missing font instead of
rendering another unwatchable clip.

**The background was near-black.** `PALETTES` values were chosen on a bright monitor
and then a 0.52 vignette was stacked on top: covers measured **lum 47/255, std 11.7**,
which is why the art read as "no background". Raised the anchors, vignette to 0.28,
and stopped the Shorts chain adding another `-0.22` brightness. Now **98/255, std 25.5**.
Old Short frame was lum 20.7; new is 59.8.

**Verifying text without eyes.** Render a frame and mask bright pixels in the lyric
band: tofu is a solid rectangle (every inked row the same width), Thai has a tall
ascender plus thin tone-mark rows. Also visible in the ASCII dump: tone marks show as
a detached short row above the base glyphs. `test_thai_font` pins this — it fails
cleanly (5 checks) when `fonts/` is moved away.

**YouTube cannot swap a file under an id.** A bad clip is fixed by upload-then-delete,
in that order, or a failed upload leaves the channel with nothing.
`music938-1/scripts/replace_video.py` does it: upload + confirm the new id, re-add to
the playlist (a new id is not the old one), **remove the retired id from the playlist**
(a delete leaves a `Deleted video` placeholder), delete the old video, and **append** a
new ledger row rather than editing the old `at` — `uploads_today()` counts rows, so
editing in place would hide the spent slot from the quota guard. A DELETE must carry
**no body** (`api_request` serialises `None` to the literal `null` → 400).
Used on song 01: `oliw1meKfcQ` → `ASuxGEvJBlw` (public, in playlist PLeA2Vf8N8O5c).

**Quota reality:** 6 uploads per Pacific day, and all 6 were spent on 2026-10-30, so

Shorts (`ANwgi7J_ras`, `JId5J48t4a4`, `CXRH7sW6Gcw`) are still the broken render;

## music938-1: competitor scan → five measurable fixes

Built `music938-1/scripts/competitor_scan.py` (quota-frugal YouTube Data API
scanner: search.list 100 units + videos.list 1 unit per 50). Ran 8 Thai queries
(เพลงไทย คาราโอเกะ / เพลงไทยเพราะ / เพลงเศร้าไทย / เพลงเพื่อชีวิต / เพลงไทยใหม่ /
คาราโอเกะ เพลงไทย ตัดเสียงร้อง / เพลงลูกทุ่ง / เพลงไทย ฟังสบาย) for **885 units**,
landing **43 channels / 1,546 videos** in `work/competitors.json`; own channel in
`work/self-scan.json` (3 units). Queries are in `competitor-queries.txt`.

**The five findings, all now implemented.** Full write-up in
`music938-1/COMPETITOR-ANALYSIS.md`; STRATEGY.md carries the summary.

1. **Compilations are the watch-hour lever.** Median views: Short 1,300 (n=65),
   song 1-5min 1,410 (n=742), **compilation ≥30min 3,578 (n=573)**, p90 126,372.
   More importantly 1 h watched per view vs 0.05 h. `compile_album.py` now writes
   `compilation_title()` = `<theme> <n> เพลง (เลือกเพลงฟังได้)` and
   `compilation_description()` with a real chapter list, and refuses under
   `MIN_TRACKS = 12` (a `N เพลง` title over 1 song is a lie).
2. **`เลือกเพลงฟังได้` is the strongest single signal.** 37 winner titles (≥100k)
   carry it; **0** loser titles (<1k) do. Needs the chapters to be honest.
3. **Winner titles are short and rarely use `|`.** ≥100k: 47.9 chars, `|` 8.0%.
   <1k: 60.7 chars, `|` 38.9%. Every `| AERIS Music` suffix removed from Short and
   karaoke titles; all titles now ≤60 chars.
4. **Our art was measurably flat.** Luminance std: top-12 competitor thumbnails
   **21.7-80.1**; ours **13.4-23.9** (cover 13.4 was the worst). Brightening the
   palette did not move it — a flat frame just gets lighter. Fix = `_highlight()`
   (warm light source), deeper `_vignette()` (0.48-0.50) and `_text_band()` (dark
   band under the title). All 20 covers + 40 thumbs re-rendered to **std 41-58**.
   `covers.py` and `tests/run.py` pin the 21.7 floor.
5. **Active channels ship ~3.89 uploads/week** (quota ceiling); we are at 1.01/wk.
   Gated by catalogue size, not release discipline.
