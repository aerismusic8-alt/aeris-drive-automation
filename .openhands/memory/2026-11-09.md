## The "hum" (เสียงฮัม) fix (night)
- K on the re-rendered music-only 8 h sleep video: "มันมีเสียงฮัม เอาแต่ดนตรีสิ".
  The hum was NOT mains hum. A spectrum check (10-40 Hz at -51 dB) ruled that out.
  It was the **sustained string/choir pad** the SoundFont arrangement held under
  every bar — a chord that never decays reads as a drone.
- **How to detect a drone in code, not by ear:** per-bin *on-time*. STFT the clip,
  and for each strong bin measure the fraction of frames where it is within ~12 dB
  of its own max. The old calm bed had a bin at 522 Hz "on" 47% of the time; a
  decaying instrument is 2-7%. Also useful: fraction of short-time-RMS frames
  within +-3 dB of the median (a drone is ~80% flat, music ~33%).
- **The fix:** removed the `pad` voice from `soundfont.MOODS`. Every voice is now
  a struck/plucked GM program (music box 10, piano 0, nylon 24, harp 46, plucked
  bass 32). The chord is voiced as a **harp arpeggio** that decays; the bass is
  struck short (0.5 bar, not held the whole bar). Calm bed: loud-bin on-time
  47% -> 17%.
- **Cache-version lesson (repeat of `drone-*`):** the beds were cached as
  `library/ambient/instrument-*.wav`, so a fixed renderer would still loop the old

### The error is app-level, not expiry (the crux)
`API access blocked` (OAuthException **code 200**) is returned to `debug_token` AND `/me`
with the token as its own `access_token`. That is not a token that aged out — it is
Facebook **blocking the app/account from the Graph API**. A brand-new token minted from
the *same* app can therefore return the same error. To fix it K must look at the Meta
**App Dashboard** for a restriction/violation (and the Page's status), not only regenerate
