### Measured, and a metric that misleads

| clip | source | motion median |
| --- | --- | --- |
| all generated (1024×576 Pollinations) | 9 AI stills | 0.066 |
| photos + generated | 4 photos + 9 AI | 1.254 |
| photos only (`--photos-only`, 3840×2560) | 5 city photos | 8.692 |

The 0.066 clip is **not frozen**: a brute-force correlation of frames at t=0.4 and
t=2.8 found a clean 52 px pan (residual 2.34). The metric is a mean frame difference,
so it tracks texture as much as movement — documented in `docs/clip-standard.md` so
nobody deletes a moving clip on that number alone.
