## What shipped
`music938-1/scripts/make_film_video.py` — cuts a real film from the Internet
Archive and matches it to the song three ways:
- **casting by mood**: the lyric header `# MOOD:` picks the film (`film_for_mood`).
  epic → the rock'n'roll film, sad → the noir film.
- **cut by section**: `window_sections()` reads the song's sections, then
  `plan_by_section()` cuts a chorus at ~2× the base beat and a verse ~1.4× slower.
  Verified: chorus 21–23 cuts/s vs verse 4–6 cuts/s (4–5× faster).
- **grade by section**: `section_grade(mood, energy)` lifts the hook
  (`brightness=0.03`) and pulls the verse down (`brightness=-0.03`).
- footage is chosen by `clip_stats()` energy (bright + motion, dark frames
  rejected), so the chorus gets the film's active moments.
- the film's own audio is dropped (`-an`) so no third-party score can be claimed.

Rendered the full song `24-yut-mai-yom` (rock, epic, Em, 160 bpm, 212.5 s) from
**Rock, Rock, Rock! (1956)** — colour, `licenseurl:
creativecommons.org/publicdomain/` confirmed on archive.org metadata. 193 shots,
212.5 s, brightness 82.5, motion 34.5, 30/43 sampled frames carry colour.
The B&W boxing film (`They Made Me a Criminal`, 1939) was the earlier cut.

Guard: `tests/run.py::test_film_video` pins the casting, the per-section pacing,

### `uploadLimitExceeded` is the channel's daily cap, not the API quota
At 08:00 UTC the shared API pool reset (`videos.list` -> 200) and the vertical
Short released to **public** (`ZUFoV_y5VbQ`, PT39S, youtu.be 303). The full
film MV upload still failed `HTTP 400 uploadLimitExceeded` — the same file that
uploaded fine as the Short. So the two limits are independent:
- **API quota** (shared pool): `videos.list`, `thumbnails.set`, etc. Resets
  00:00 America/Los_Angeles = 08:00 UTC.
- **Upload cap** (`uploadLimitExceeded`): a per-channel count of *new videos per
  day*, unverified-channel territory. Clears on its own, over hours, not at the
  API reset. Do not read it as "the API is down".
`/tmp/ar/retry_mv.py` retries the MV every 20 min (a burst is what tends to
extend the cap). Note the song **already exists** on ch3 as public
(`ยืนหยัดไม่ยอม`, uploaded 2026-11-12), so the goal of "the song is on the
channel" is already met; the new file is the film MV cut of it.
