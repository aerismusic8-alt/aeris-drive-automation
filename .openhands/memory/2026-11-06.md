## K's new binding rules (session 2026-11-06)
- **Pentatonic** — "ใช้เพนทาโทนิคด้วยนะ". Every Flow style prompt now carries a
  pentatonic clause. Scale by mood/genre: sad/mystic→minor, else major.
- **Music must not repeat** — "ดนตรีห้ามซ้ำกัน". Each song needs its own
  arrangement/instrumentation, not just a new genre label.
- **5–7 syllables is NOT a rule** — K clarified "พยางค์ไม่ได้บังคับนะแค่ยกตัวอย่าง".
  Do not hard-enforce it.
- **Rework the catalogue before releasing** the 17 unreleased songs.
- **Upload songs to Google Drive** — "อัพไว้กูเกิ้ลไดร์ฟได้ไหม ... สร้างโฟลเดอร์
  ไว้กันปนกันด้วย". Use `music938-1/scripts/drive_share.py` (per-type folders).

## The diagnosis (measured + confirmed by 9/10 advisors)
The catalogue was monochrome, not badly written:
- 20/22 songs had **no `# GENRE:` header** → all fell back to one "เพื่อชีวิต"
  style. Only 21 (lukthung) and 24 (rock) were tagged.
- 21/22 **female** voice; tempo clustered **64–96** (mean 79).
- Every song: identical `V-C-V-C-B-C`, ~8 syllables/line, AABB rhyme, and the
  hook sung only **once per chorus** (in-chorus repeat = 1 for 21/22).
- chorus share 0.18, distinct-line ratio 0.64.

The advisor panel (chatgpt, 7 Duck models) all said the same thing: the problem
is **one production template across the whole catalogue**. Recommended fix:
rotate genres (lukthung/rock/pop/indie, none adjacent), spread tempo, vary the
voice, make the arrangement carry identity, use pentatonic as the melodic core.

## What shipped
- `channel/arrangements.json` — 24 signatures (6 per genre). `scripts/arrangement.py`
  assigns one per song: tagged songs first inside their own genre pool, header-less
  (released) songs take the rest. `--check` fails on a shared id. Two-pass matters:
  a single greedy pass lets a header-less song starve a genre.
- `flow_song.style_for` appends the pentatonic clause + the song's arrangement to
  every prompt, with verbatim-clause dedupe (`_dedupe_clauses`).
- `scripts/rework_catalog.py` — sets GENRE/TEMPO/VOICE on the **17 unreleased**
  songs (rotation lukthung 5/rock 5/indie 4/pop 3, tempo 76–160, voice 10 male/
  7 female), leaves the **5 released** songs byte-identical (they are reverted
  with `git checkout` after any run; the script itself restores their headers).
- `channel/genres.json` — new `pop` genre; `genres.PALETTE_FOR["pop"]="hopeful"`.
- `scripts/analyze_songs.py` — quantitative songbook report (hook repeat/share,
  chorus %, distinct, syllable SD, rhyme).
- `scripts/run_panel.py` — advisor driver that **saves after each answer**;
  `consult10.py` only writes `--out` at the end, so one hung advisor lost nine
  answers (exit 2 at advisor 10, nothing saved).
- `scripts/drive_share.py` — Drive uploader that pulls media then uploads to
  per-type folders under `AX Content Lab/music938/AERIS Music — คลังเพลง/`.
- `ops/phone_login.py` — added a Drive surface button.
- tests: +2 suites, **1024 pass, 0 fail**.
