## The picture gate, and what it found in the surreal lane

K's "หน้าปกเพี้ยน" was two defects on one surface. Both are now measured by code.

**A blank picture can no longer ship.** `clip_standard.picture()` and
`thumb_standard.measure()` both read the middle 32–68 % band — the part that is
picture, between the label and the ask — and record its luma std plus the % of
the band within ±6 of its median. Every earlier rule (range, edges, ink) passes
on a grey panel with a title and a caption on it: four shipped surreal covers
measured std 0.00 and passed anyway. Floors: clip 3.0 / 92 % one tone, thumbnail
4.0 / 80 % flat rows. `make_short.thumb_for` now samples five frames, keeps the
most detailed one and **deletes the clip** when none of them has a picture.

**ffmpeg facts that cost a render each** (all verified at 1080x1920):

- `rotate=...:fillcolor=black@0` on a yuv frame is **opaque black**; the alpha is
  dropped. Every rotation therefore paints black corners, and `bar_frac` reads
  them as wasted bars. The fix is a margin: a 9:16 frame rotated by θ stays
  covered only above `1080 / (1080·cosθ − 1920·sinθ)` (6° → 1.24, 7.5° → 1.32).
  Scale past it and crop back; `RECIPE_MARGIN` in `make_surreal_short.py`.
- `geq` reading `r(X+8*sin(...))` past the right edge returns **black**, so a
  colour wave painted a 33 % black band down the side. Clamp it:
  `r(clip(X+8*sin(...),0,W-1),Y)`.
- A landscape cover cropped to 9:16 **slices its own printed title** ("...กไม้ริมทา").
  The lane now shows the whole cover centred over a blurred copy of itself.
- That blurred backdrop must not be pure black or the top rows are a "bar": the
  letterbox of a dark cover measured 7.4 % black at `brightness=-0.10` and 0 % at
  `+0.08`. The scrim over it is `black@0.30`.
- `negate` on the whole frame makes the thumbnail 95 % bright, which the
  thumbnail floor refuses as a failed flood fill. The glitch recipe keeps its
  chroma tear and drops the inversion; a 1.5° rotation supplies the motion.
- `load_lyrics` lowercases its header keys and `release.parse_lyrics` does not, so
  reading `meta["TITLE"]` off the first always missed and every surreal/concert
  Short printed the slug as the song name. `make_karaoke.song_title()` reads both.
