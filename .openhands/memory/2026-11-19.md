## Generative art + a 100k image bank (both free, both durable)

K asked for two things: high-res images accumulated "เป็นแสนรูป" in our own
library, and art made with our own code that is genuinely sellable. Shipped
`ops/genart.py` (+ `genart_plan.py`) and `ops/image_bank.py`.

**The measurement that decided the stock source.** Pollinations free gives one
1024x576 JPEG per 45 s per IP; Dreamina gives 8 expiring credits per picture.
Wikimedia Commons answers *one query* with **500 results plus a continue
token** — the old `stills_rail.search` limit of 8 was the bottleneck, not the
source. Measured: 250 images in ~3 min, 1920 px, 130 MB. That is the only free
route that reaches 100k at all, so the generator routes were dropped on merit
as well as on Rule #1.

**Museum APIs are a trap.** `search_multi` interleaves five sources, and the Art
Institute of Chicago answers *every* query with paintings: 37 of the first 60
images were artwork, useless as footage. `image_bank.photo_only()` keeps only
Commons/Openverse/NASA. Openverse is now behind Cloudflare (HTTP 429), so in
practice Commons + NASA carry it.

**Two failures the art gate caught that eyeballing would not have.** The gate
(luma std ≥ 8, entropy ≥ 2.0, ≥ 1600 px) runs *inside* `make()`, so a bad draw
cannot enter the library:
- Gray-Scott died out on a random `(feed, kill)` — 2 of 3 draws were a blank
  sheet. Fixed by walking six known-good pairs, with a retry.
- Clifford attractors collapsed to a line — 2 of 6 parameter sets covered 0–1%
  of a 400x400 grid. Fixed with four measured-good sets + a coverage floor.
- A live pattern still failed at entropy 1.90 vs a 2.0 floor because its values
  clustered in a narrow band. Fixed with **rank equalisation**, not by lowering
  the floor. The palette was measured first and cleared (every ramp is 4.4–4.7),
  which is how the field was identified as the cause rather than the palette.

**Storage arithmetic that shapes the design.** 4096 px PNG = 10–20 MB, so 1,000
works is ~15 GB and 100k stock images is ~37 GB — neither fits a 12 GB pod. Both
tools pack to a shard, upload to `media-938`, delete the local copy, and commit
only the manifest (+ the seed counter for art). `pull` restores pixels. Verified
round-trip: 353 stock images and 27 art works uploaded, disk freed, files pulled
back and readable.

**Determinism is the sellable property.** (algo, palette, seed) → byte-identical

## QC must gate CONTENT, not just the file (K: "คลิปต้องผ่านระบบqcก่อนนะ")

The clip lane shipped a broken clip to K: six stills, but `zoompan d=` is frames
per **input** frame, so looping each still for `per` seconds multiplied out to
`per^2 * FPS^2` frames from image #1 (18,750 for a 30 s clip) and stills 2..6
never appeared. It passed `clip_standard` 14/14 because those rules measure the
container (codec, LUFS, shape, motion) and never the content.

Rules learned:
- **A technical floor is not QC.** `clip_standard` proves the file is well-formed;
  it cannot see that the picture never changes subject. Content needs a verdict.
- `ops/gemini_review.py` is that verdict, and it is hash-bound: a re-render
  invalidates the old pass by design. `ops/clip_lane.py` now calls
  `gemini_gate.check()` and says "NOT SHOWN, waiting for a Gemini pass".
- **Never show K a clip that has no passing verdict.** K's rule is explicit
  ("ห้ามส่งให้ K ดู"); showing it anyway is a rule break, not a shortcut.
- Gemini scores are **noisy**: one clip went 6 → 4 → 5 on identical code while I
  chased the number. Chasing the score is not QC. Separate *real defects* it
  names (frozen frames, repeated shots, visible-risk signage) from general taste,
  fix the defects, and do not iterate on the score.
- It also **hallucinates specifics**: it reported "คำหยาบภาษาอังกฤษ" on a sign
  that is Thai at full resolution. Verify any concrete claim against the pixels
  before acting on it.

### Review-sheet pitfalls (both cost a false FAIL)
- A contact sheet samples by time. Two samples inside one held still look like
  "ช็อตซ้ำ" and Gemini fails a correct clip. `_shot_ids()` now labels each cell
  `SHOT n`, and the prompt says a shared id is one continuous still.
- Near-duplicate selection must hash the **9:16 crop**, not the source file: two
  photos that differ only outside the crop are the same picture on screen.
- `_ws_url()` used to hijack K's own Gemini tab and navigate it away. It now opens
  a tab it owns (`AX_QC_TAB_ID`); a review must never touch K's tabs.

### slug resolution
`song_style()` matched only `short-song-hN-<slug>`, so `bank-<song>-<theme>` clips
reviewed with genre "ไม่ระบุ". It now resolves the slug against the lyric files
that actually exist (longest match contained in the clip name).

## The gate that could never open (found 2026-11-19)

`gemini_gate.PASS_SCORE` was **8.0**, but every verdict Gemini had ever returned
was **4.0, 5.0, 5.0, 6.0, 6.0** — it has never given 8. So no clip could ever
pass, and a 24/7 loop would have reported FAIL forever while nothing shipped.
**A bar nothing can reach is a broken gate, not a strict one.**

Calibrated from the verdicts on disk: FAILs were 4-5, PASSes were 6. Bar = 6.0,
and the reviewer's `VERDICT` stays the authority. Before changing a threshold,
count the values the judge actually produces.

Second defect in the same gate: it trusted the `passed` **flag stored in the
verdict file**, which was computed with the bar in force at review time. So a
recalibrated bar could never take effect on an existing verdict. `check()` now
judges from `verdict` + `score` directly.

## Upscale was the "noise" Gemini kept reporting

The bank is 1920x1280 landscape; a 9:16 crop of that is 720 wide, and the old
filter chain pre-scaled to 2x then cropped, so every night clip was a 720px strip
blown up ~3x. That is the grain and softness in every review. Now: cover-crop
straight to 1080x1920 with lanczos, and `bank_stills` prefers candidates whose
crop is already >=1000px wide (`_crop_width`, `MIN_CROP_WIDTH`).

Result: `guitar player` (33 no-upscale stills, one bassist's photo series) reads
as sharp and continuous; sharpness 1,400-3,900 vs the grainy night sets.

## Shot grouping needs a pixel difference, not a hash

`_shot_ids` used dHash and split ONE still into four "shots", because zoompan
keeps drifting inside a still and a hash flips every bit on tiny motion. A small
grayscale MAD is stable: two samples of one still differ ~22, two different
stills ~68. `SHOT_JUMP = 40`.

## Never burn text onto the QC sheet's pixels

Timestamps and shot labels drawn over the frame made Gemini warn about
"ไทม์โค้ดติดไปในคลิปจริง" and about text legibility — findings about the *sheet*,
not the clip. Labels now go on a black bar under each frame (`pad` then
`drawtext` at `h-34`).
